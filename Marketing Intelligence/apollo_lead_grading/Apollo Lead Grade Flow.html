Prepare the company list

Rules / transform
  • Collapses contacts to one row per company, keyed on the Apollo account id.
  • Cleans names ("MIRACL | The login you love" → MIRACL; ALL CAPS → Title Case).
  • Uses the email domain when there is no website.
  • Renamed companies: a name like "Monarch Healthcare Now RDCP Care" is split into old and new, but only when the website, an email domain or the LinkedIn page (or another lead in the list) matches one of the two names. The new name is searched. With no evidence the name is left alone and flagged for a check.
  • Companies with a funding label get a 365-day news lookback so the news can date it.
  • Applies overrides.csv: aliases, context words, and "ambiguous" flags for generic names.
  • Writes a separate parents.csv for the 13 parent companies. No contact data is written.

NLP enrichment of each article

Machine learning
  • spaCy named entities: for small companies, rejects headlines where the name is part of another entity ("Array Technologies") or tagged as a person or place.
  • spaCy dependency parse: extracts the event and who did what to whom ("X acquires Y" vs "X acquired by Y"), amounts, places, executive names, certainty.
  • MiniLM sentence embeddings: how close the article is to the company's website description, and grouping the same story from several outlets into one.
  • Cached per story, so each story is processed once. CPU only, ~20 s for this list.

5

Judge the match and classify the event

RulesMachine learning
  • Is it really this company? Match score ≥ 0.6 plus identity checks; a volume check re-judges short names with 15+ matches and little evidence. Rejected articles go to review.csv. In this run 801 matched, 951 rejected.
  • What kind of event? Two judges:
    • Keyword rules (classify.py)
    • A logistic regression over mpnet sentence embeddings, trained on example headlines in event_examples.py with the company name masked as "Acme"
  • Confidence: high = both agree; medium = rules only; low = classifier only.
  • Categories: Funding, PE buyout, M&A, Expansion, Restructuring, Contract / partnership win, Leadership change, Product launch, Other.

Review loops

Human reviewLLM
  • A person or Claude checks the output and records fixes in CSVs that the next run reads:
    • overrides.csv: generic names that matched someone else ("The Fed", "Titania")
    • event_overrides.csv: an event given the wrong type
    • manual_events.csv: an event the classifier missed, with labelled_by
  • Phrasings that will recur are taught to the classifier (event_examples.py). eval/eval_events.py then checks precision and recall didn't drop.
  • Claude labelled the 9 manual events and the eval gold labels (865 stories). No person has checked those labels yet.

Turn register records into dated events

Rules
  • Takeover (M&A): a CQC location registered under the lead within 90 days of a location at the same postcode closing under another provider.
  • Expansion: a location registered under the lead with no such predecessor. A home moving between the lead's own companies is ignored.
  • Leadership change: a different registered manager from last month's snapshot. Shown but not scored, because turnover at one home is common.
  • Contract / partnership win: each public contract award.
  • These go in the same queue as the hand-labelled events, at full strength and labelled with their source. The strongest event still decides.

Join internal data

Internal dataMachine learningLLM
  • Priority list (MyStaff + HubSpot): does the website or email domain belong to an active client?
  • Client Propensity prospect profiles: logistic regression models trained on past Cloudstaff clients give each industry's chance of growing to 5+ seats in 1 year or 10+ in 2 years, and of adding a second department. All countries are used, since there are only 26 UK clients.
  • Roles we fill (roles.py): each job title posted is matched to Staff Central JP titles with MiniLM similarity (≥ 0.80 we fill, 0.62–0.80 possible).
  • Competitor-client map: competitor → client pairs extracted by rules from candidates' CVs (18,302 rows). Uncertain rows are reviewed by a person, with a local Qwen 4B LLM (Ollama) pre-filling suggestions.
  • UK market trend: context only, not scored.

Target-industry check, sub-sector and gates

Rules
  • Target check uses evidence in order, because Apollo's industry label is often wrong: Companies House SIC for furniture or building trades, then Apollo SIC / NAICS activity codes, then website service lines, then Apollo's industry.
  • Gates before scoring:
    • Distressed (administration, liquidation, strike-off) → Excluded
    • Active client → Existing client
    • Renamed to another lead in the list → Duplicate
    • Not in the target industry → Check fit: scored but not tiered
  • Sub-sector (descriptive, no points): from CQC service types when registered, else the activity codes. Care: nursing, residential, learning disability / mental health, home care, staffing agency. Cyber: services, software product, managed services, training. Each comes with a pitch hint.

Priority Score (out of 100)

Rules
  • The live Priority Score code (priority_scoring.py, profile dashboard_v3, New Logo) without the HubSpot parts. Their 40 points are shared out across the rest.
  • News: events add up. Reports of the same kind within 30 days are grouped into one event (news, hand-labelled, CQC or contract). Each is worth strength × recency; they combine as 1 − (1−a)(1−b)…, capped at the 25 points. M&A strength is 0.8; 31–90 days old is ×0.7.
  • Fit: 75% growth (High = full) + 25% second department.
  • Act now bonus: +15 per trigger (fresh funding or buyout, hiring spike, fresh restructuring or leadership news). It only fires from the event that is scored.

Tier within the Apollo list

Rules
  • Ranked within each list (care and cyber are separate campaigns): top 10% Tier 1, next 30% Tier 2, the rest Tier 3.
  • Ties are broken by readiness (0–4, one point each): an Apollo funding label · a decision-maker with a verified email · news in the last 180 days · in the target industry. Readiness never adds points.

Write outputs and build the dashboard

Output
  • grade_leads.py writes lead_grades_apollo_emea.xlsx (leads, contacts, news) and .json (the page's data). Both stay out of the repo because they hold contact names and emails.
  • build_dashboard.py embeds the JSON in dashboard_template.html to make one offline HTML file. check_page.py must report errors: [].
  • The page has a leads table (Why chips, readiness), a lead panel, and pages for data gathered, fit and market, how grading works, and QA. Filters include sub-sector.

Where the models are

  • spaCy entities and dependency parse (identity, event details)
  • MiniLM embeddings (website fit, story de-duplication, job title → Cloudstaff role)
  • mpnet embeddings + logistic regression (headline event classifier)
  • Logistic regression on past clients (Client Propensity fit)

Where LLMs are

  • Claude: labelled missed events in manual_events.csv and the eval gold labels, in a working session, not during a run
  • Qwen 4B (local, Ollama): pre-fills suggestions for the competitor-client review queue
  • Possible next step: a local LLM for medium and low confidence events only

What's rules-only

  • News match score, keyword event rules, Companies House matching
  • Target check, gates, Priority Score, tiers and readiness
  • Rename split, CQC takeover rule, contract matching, sub-sectors
  • Every threshold is a setting at the top of grade_leads.py

Public registers used

  • Companies House and the Gazette: status, SIC, accounts, insolvency
  • CQC (England's care regulator): locations, beds, services, ratings; monthly files
  • Find a Tender: UK public contract awards; a year is cached, later runs fetch only new months

Known gaps

  • CQC covers England only (not Scotland, Wales or Northern Ireland)
  • Contracts Finder and the devolved portals aren't read; care spot placements get no notice
  • Care contracts can be published up to about 4 months after signing
