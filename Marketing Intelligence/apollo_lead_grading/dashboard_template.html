Grade by Apollo list

leads
Tiers are ranks within each Apollo list (separate campaigns): the top 10% of a list are Tier 1, the next 30% Tier 2. Equal scores and readiness share the higher tier, so a tier can be larger than its share. Check fit = outside the list's target industry, scored but not tiered. Excluded = in administration, liquidation or strike-off.

Priority score distribution

leads per score band
Most leads sit in a narrow band because little is known about them yet: no website activity, and news for only a few. The spread grows as signals are added.

Lead readiness

leads by readiness points (0–4), by list
Readiness breaks ties between equal scores and never adds points. One point each: an Apollo funding label, a decision-maker with a verified email, news or own-website posts in the last 180 days, and being in the list's target industry.

News: what the search found

companies, by list
"Found" = at least one article confirmed to be about the company (including its own news page). "Checked, nothing found" is a real zero. "Not checked" means the search failed or didn't run, so it counts as unknown.

News events found

strongest event per company
One event per company: the high-confidence event first, then the strongest by type × recency. Medium and low confidence count at half strength in the score.

Companies House and the Gazette

companies, by list
Matched by exact legal name in the November Companies House file. "Not found" is mostly a trading name that differs from the legal name, not a missing company.

Careers pages and job feeds

companies, by list
Only "ok" gives a count of open roles. "No roles found" often means the page loads its list with JavaScript or links to a job board, so it is unknown, not zero.

Open roles, in bands

companies whose careers page was read, by list
Roles open on the company's own careers page or job system on the day of the run. Job boards (Indeed, LinkedIn, NHS Jobs) are not read, so a company can be hiring and still show "no open roles" here.

How recent the hiring is

companies with open roles, by newest posting
From each role's posted date. The score counts roles posted in the last 30 days (20+ = full hiring points); older open roles show as a reason but earn no points.

Apollo funding against the news

companies with an Apollo label, by type
Apollo gives the type of the latest funding but no date or amount. "Matches the news" = the news found the same kind of event (e.g. Apollo M&A, news: bought by…). "News shows a different event" = both have something, but not the same (e.g. Apollo Debt Financing, news: PE buyout). "Not in the news" = nothing to date it. Apollo funding earns no points either way; how to reconcile the two is still open.

Contacts in the export

people, by seniority and list
All emails in these exports are marked Verified by Apollo (ZeroBounce). Decision-makers = owner, founder, C-suite, partner, VP or director.

Coverage by source

companies in the current filters
Every source is recorded per company, so a failed check never reads as "nothing found".

Fit profile used, by industry

Client Propensity, industry only
The chance that a new client like this reaches 5+ seats in a year, 10+ in two years, or adds a second department in a year, from past Cloudstaff clients in the same industry (all countries). Bands are relative: High = top third of past new clients.

Market trend (context only)

News Scraper segment mode · not in the score
How UK news for each sector leans, from News Scraper segment mode: 0.5 is neutral, above means more growth, labour-shortage and offshoring stories. Context only, not in the score: tiers rank within each list, where every lead shares the same trend, so it could not change anyone\'s rank.

Leads by industry

Apollo industry → Cloudstaff industry group
Target industry is decided by activity codes (Apollo US SIC / NAICS and the Companies House UK SIC), with Apollo's one-word industry only as a fallback: e.g. Coxbench Hall Residential Care Home is "individual & family services" in Apollo but SIC 8052 (nursing care). Companies whose codes show another trade (furniture, building, staffing, design) are "Check fit".
