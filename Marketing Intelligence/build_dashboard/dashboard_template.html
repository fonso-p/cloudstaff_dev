Signals at a glance

most points each signal can add, out of 100
Points = the most a signal can add; partial activity earns part of them. On both New Logo and Organic Growth the signals add up to 100 points, so the points are also the signal's share of the score (20 points = 20%). The industry trend then multiplies the total, and the trigger bonus is added on top, with the score rounded and capped at 100. The yellow column shows what is used when data is missing. When website, news and jobs, or seat growth data is missing, the account gets a yellow "No … data" chip on the list.

What you will see on the list

The Priority Score answers one question: how worth pursuing is this account right now? Every account goes through the same six steps. Every weight and threshold below is read from the scoring settings.

The Priority Score in six steps

one number from 0 to 100
Priority score = (Website points + News and jobs points + Seat growth points + Referral points + Competitor client points) × Industry nudge + Trigger bonus, rounded to a whole number and capped at 100

Most points each signal can add

by list
Partial activity earns part of the points, for example fewer visits than it takes to get full credit. The two lists weigh things differently, because the flags only apply to prospects.

How much each type of news counts

strength from 0 to 1
0 = no effect, 1 = full credit. Only the strongest news item counts.

How recency fades news

multiplies the news strength
Age of the news item on the as-of date.

How seat growth counts

Organic Growth only · share of the seat growth points
Seats the client added in the last 6 months. More seats added means a higher signal, so a client that keeps expanding ranks higher. Unknown seat history is scored with the average of the clients that have it (see step 3).

Where the accounts go

source files to the five lists
Each account has one owner role (Marketing, SDR or BDM) and sits on that role's New Logo or Organic Growth list. SDR owns New Logo accounts only. Accounts come from HubSpot (New Logo) and MyStaff (Organic Growth), merged by the format_priority_data pipeline. Owner roles follow the pipeline's routing (format data/BUSINESS_RULES.md §11.3): a trigger event decides the team, and other accounts follow the pipeline's routing mode (see the banner).

Unknown data by indicator

share of accounts with no data
All accounts. Referral and competitor client flags apply to New Logo only, and seat growth to Organic Growth only. Unknown website and news data are scored with the average of the accounts that have the data (see How the score works).

What the rounded points and seat growth change

tier counts, workbook settings vs. the points in this dashboard
Both scores are before the trigger bonus. This dashboard uses round point values (multiples of 5), for unknown signals, and adds seat growth for Organic Growth. "csv" = the same accounts scored with the original workbook settings.

What the trigger bonus changes

tier counts, before the bonus vs. with the bonus
Only accounts with a trigger event get the bonus. Lists follow the owner role, so the bonus changes tiers and order within a list, never the list itself.
