What you’ll see on the list

Priority score

One number from 0 to 100 for how worth pursuing an account is right now. Tier 1 is — or more, Tier 2 is — or more, and everything below is Tier 3.

Act now

Accounts with a time-sensitive trigger, such as a form fill or a Diamond referral. They’re pinned to the top of the list whatever their score, because timing matters more than overall fit.

Estimated data

When we don’t have website or news data for an account, its score uses a typical level instead. A yellow “No … data” chip shows which part was estimated, so you know the score is based on a partial picture.

Lists

BDM · New Logo holds prospects with a Diamond referral, an Act now trigger or a score of — or more. Organic Growth holds existing clients for Account Managers. Each account appears on one list only. The SDR list isn’t shown in this mock.

The Priority Score answers one question: how worth pursuing is this account right now? Every account goes through the same five steps.

Priority score = (Website points + News and jobs points + Referral points + Prior BPO points) × Industry nudge
