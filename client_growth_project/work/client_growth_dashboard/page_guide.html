Client Growth Dashboard: page guide

How to read each page and the filters · clients first staffed from Jan 2020

What the dashboard is

One page per question about where, when and how clients grow. Growth = net seats: staff who started minus staff who left. Seats come from the SC Engagement List; region, Custom Division, industry and Active come from MyStaff. Internal and test accounts are excluded and listed on the QA page.

The filter bar has two rows

Which clients (always applies) Region (APAC, AMER incl. EMEA, OPTI), Custom division, Industry roll-up, Status and Path to 5 seats (headcount). They pick the clients you see, on every page.
How growth is measured (only where it matters) Window length (3, 5, 6, 12 months), Net seats added in a window (3+, 5+, 10+, 20+), Starting segment and Window start year. Shown only on pages that use them; on Seasonality only the year shows, as Calendar year.
Two different "5"s. Path to 5 seats is about size: did the account ever have 5 staff at the same time, and how fast. Net seats added in a window is about a growth spurt: did the account add, say, 5 net seats inside any 6-month stretch of its life. A client can be "Fast to 5" without ever adding 5 net seats in one window, for example by launching with 4.

Key definitions

Window
A block of the client's own life: window 1 = its first N months, window 2 = the next N. Complete windows only.
Window net
Staff at the window end minus staff at its start; window 1 starts at the end of month 1, so launch seats are not growth.
Starting seats
Staff at the end of the client's first month.
Peak seats
Most staff on the books on any single day.
Fast / Slow to 5
5 staff at the same time within 6 months (183 days) of the first start, or later.
Status
MyStaff Active plus staff today: active with staff, active with 0 staff, inactive.

Reading the results

  • "Differs", "higher" or "lowest" only when a test gives p < 0.05; otherwise "no clear difference".
  • Groups under 20 are greyed and flagged; only groups of 30+ are named highest or lowest.
  • These are associations in the data, not causes.
  • The latest month is a partial month.
  • Not available in the source files: company size, and gross margin or account economics.
  • Out of scope: clients whose first staff started before 2020, and accounts tagged VAR in MyStaff (see the QA page for their headcount).
